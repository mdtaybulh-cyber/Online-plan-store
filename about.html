About Blooming Oasis

Growing happiness one plant at a time.

Our Story

Blooming Oasis was founded by two lifelong plant lovers who believed everyone deserves to experience the joy of living with nature. What started as a small balcony garden quickly grew into an online plant shop dedicated to helping people build greener, healthier homes.

Our mission is simple: Bring nature indoors while making plant care easy and enjoyable for everyone.

Eco-Friendly Sourcing

We partner with trusted local growers who use responsible cultivation methods and reduce environmental impact. Every order is packed using recyclable boxes, biodegradable wrapping materials, and reusable packaging whenever possible.
